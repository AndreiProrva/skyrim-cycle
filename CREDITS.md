**AndreiProrva** — project concept, creative direction, bicycle design choices,
generation and selection of component models, sound selection, in-game testing,
feedback and supplied gameplay screenshots.

Meshy licensing reference: https://docs.meshy.ai/en/webapp/pricing.

**OpenAI Codex** — AI-generated implementation code, tooling, technical model
assembly/adaptation, animation/rig work and documentation, iteratively directed
and tested by AndreiProrva. Codex is the development agent; GPT refers to the
underlying model family. No claim is made that a single named model performed
every step of the project. The mod includes AI-generated code and assets.

**TiesWijnen — Bicycle.aif**, Freesound, sound 337078, **CC0 1.0**:
https://freesound.org/people/TiesWijnen/sounds/337078/.
Original pack supplied by the author:
https://freesound.org/people/TiesWijnen/packs/19082/.
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/.
The recording was trimmed, converted to mono PCM WAV, looped and gain-adjusted
for the starting/rolling bicycle sound; playback pitch varies modestly with
cadence. The separately supplied stopping recording is not included or used
in this version. No synthetic voice or cloned actor voice is included.

including its Skyrim 1.7.104 support. Preserve COPYING.txt, EXCEPTIONS.md and
third-party notices; the old MIT notice alone is not the current license.
Upstream: https://github.com/alandtse/CommonLibSSE-NG.

**SKSE team; Address Library authors** — required separately installed

**fmt, spdlog, Microsoft DirectXTK/DirectXMath, rapidcsv and OpenVR contributors** —
